Your ads update in real time. Your clinic pages update by lantern light.

The DTC Funnel: Rewired is an illustrated field guide to the most expensive blind spot in medtech marketing: the handoff from your ad to the clinic.

At the top of your funnel, everything moves at the speed of mission control. Bids change every second, creative is tested every week, and the dashboards glow.

At the bottom, where the patient finally decides to book, it’s a different century: broken locator links, clinic pages that never mention your device, and “call during business hours.”

Your funnel is only as fast as its slowest layer. This book shows where yours slows down and what to do about it.
